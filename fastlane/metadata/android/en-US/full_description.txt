VanshVriksh is an offline, local-first family tree and genealogy app.

Build and explore your family history: add people and relationships, record life events, attach photos and media, and view your tree as an interactive diagram or a fan chart.

Features
* Interactive family tree with generation-based layout
* Fan chart view for a compact overview of ancestors
* Person profiles with facts, events, relationships, photos and media
* Multiple family trees
* GEDCOM import
* Upcoming birthdays dashboard
* Duplicate detection and merging
* Data integrity checks
* Research notes for tracking your findings
* Encrypted local backups that you can export, share and restore

Your data stays on your device
VanshVriksh stores everything locally. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Backups are ZIP files (optionally AES-encrypted) that you can share with any app — including cloud storage of your choice.

Permissions
* Media/photo access: used to attach pictures to family members.
* Location (optional): only used if you choose to record a place with an event; you can skip it entirely.
